Privacy Policy

When you request an appointment, Akkis Salon collects your name, phone number, selected service, preferred date and time, and optional message. We use this information only to respond to your request, manage appointments, and provide salon services.

We do not sell booking information. We keep records only as long as reasonably needed for appointments, accounting, and legal obligations. Contact us to request correction or deletion of your information.

By submitting a booking request, you agree that Akkis Salon may contact you about that request by phone, SMS, WhatsApp, or email.
